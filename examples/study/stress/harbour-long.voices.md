# Corin
- Terse. Drops the subject of a sentence: "Got the oar?", not "Have you got the oar?"
- Sailor's words: slip, cargo, aft, mate.
- Calls the player by the name they gave him.
- Superstitious: touches wood, and will not say "drown" or "sea" after dark.
- Never says thank you. Says "owe you one" instead.
> Honest names float.
> Boat leaves at dark.
