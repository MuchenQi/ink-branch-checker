# Tamsin
- Talks fast. Long run-on sentences joined with "and" and "because".
- Local words: "aye", "proper", "grand". Never formal.
- Asks questions and answers them herself.
- Says "sir" only to people she fears or is mocking.
- When frightened she talks more, not less, and jokes when things are bad.
> Sit anywhere that isn't on fire, and if it is on fire, aye, tell me.
> No coin. Grand. Lovely.
