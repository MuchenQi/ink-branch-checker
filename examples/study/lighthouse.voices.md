# Odile
- Formal and measured. Long, complete sentences. Never uses contractions.
- Speaks of the sea as "she", as if it were a person.
- Calls the player "courier". She does not use warmer names.
- Dry, understated humour. She never exclaims.
- When she distrusts someone she becomes more polite, not less. Cold courtesy, never insults.
- Does not speak of her son unless she trusts the listener.
> You have come a long way to stand in my doorway, courier.
> The sea does not like visitors on her rocks.
